Style guide

Principles of the line.

Every illustration in TALA is built from the same eight rules. If a frame breaks one, it's not Sol — it's something else.

The eight rules.

01 · One stroke weight
All outlines are 2.5–3.5px, scaled with the artwork. Never mix thick and thin in the same frame.
02 · Round caps and joins
Every line end is round. Square caps belong in industrial tools, not financial companions.
03 · Two-color body, max
Sol is one fill color + one accent (cheek/highlight). Background scenery uses a third at most.
04 · Face minimum
Two dot eyes, one mouth curve. No eyebrows. No noses. Mood lives in the mouth, eye shape, and pose.
05 · Limbs are sticks
Arms and legs (when Sol has them) are 2.5px lines with 5px circle hands/feet. Never tapered.
06 · Negative space is warm
Scene backgrounds are cream #FAF8F5 or pastel tints — never white.
07 · Decoration is sparse
3–6 confetti shapes per celebration, not 60. Less is louder.
08 · Export at 2× & 3×
Every illustration ships at 1×, 2×, 3× PNG plus the original SVG. iOS asset catalog naming.

Reward illustrations · Three moments

Quietly bigger, each time.

The onboarding pathway has three reward beats. Each one is louder than the last — but none are loud enough to feel like a sales pitch.

Reward 1 · First number in.

Single solar spark from the ring centre. 280ms ease-out. The smallest possible "thank you".

Reward 2 · Goal locked in.

GoalCard slides up (y:40→0) at 520ms spring, two solar particles, Sol peeks. Medium-loud.

Reward 3 · Pathway complete.

Full overlay. Sol-celebrating, 60 confetti particles, sunrise gradient sweep, score reveals digit-by-digit. Auto-dismiss 4.5s.

Production notes

For the developer.

File set & naming

  • SVG source/assets/illustrations/sol-{state}.svg · /assets/badges/badge-{slug}.svg
  • PNG raster1×, 2×, 3× — [email protected], [email protected], [email protected]
  • React component<Sol state="celebrating" size={120} /> — single component, state prop drives the variant
  • Stroke uniformityAll paths use vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke" so 24px and 240px sizes share line weight
  • ColorsHard-coded to the brand tokens. Never use named colors (e.g. "orange") in the SVG.
  • Animation hooksEach Sol SVG has <g id="rays">, <g id="face">, <g id="arms"> so Framer can target parts independently
  • ConfettiNot in SVG. Use canvas-confetti or framer-motion particles overlay — keeps SVGs render-time cheap.
  • Dark modeSol body shifts to navy-100, rays to solar-400, line work stays navy-900. One palette swap, no asset duplication.
